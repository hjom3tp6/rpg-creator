# Structure · Conspiracy Mystery — pull one thread, find the web
cast_pattern: rotating

A hidden truth is reshaping the world, and someone is working to keep it hidden. The player starts
with one loose thread and keeps pulling until the whole web is visible — and has to decide what to do
with what they find. (This was the old default shape; it is now just one option among many.)

## Phase shape (3 acts)
1. **The thread** — an inciting oddity (a body, a missing file, a lie that doesn't fit). The player
   commits to finding out. Ends on the first sign that someone powerful noticed.
2. **The web** — clues widen into factions and motives. A midpoint reveal reframes what the player
   thought they were investigating. Allies prove compromised; the hunter becomes the hunted.
3. **The closing net** — the conspiracy moves to bury the truth and the player at once. Every planted
   clue converges in one pressured confrontation over who controls the truth.

## Pressure (the ticking clock, in its classic form)
A dated deadline (the ritual, the vote, the shipment) plus pursuit: the conspiracy closes in as the
player gets closer. Keep at least one clock in `clock.deadlines` and make its approach felt.

## Opposition
A hidden network with an off-screen mover — a person behind an institution. It acts between scenes
(witnesses vanish, records change, a friendly face reports back).

## Endings it supports
Exposure at a cost · bittersweet (truth out, someone lost) · the player joins or inherits the web ·
open/ambiguous (the truth is only half-told) · tragic (the cover-up wins, but someone remembers).

## Cast pattern — rotating ensemble
Sub-casts change beat to beat (`A+B`, `A+D`, `E` solo); every major character gets a two-hander;
full cast only at the climax. The ensemble-depth gate applies in full.

## What a good beat looks like
A clue is earned through a person, not a lore dump; the answer is partial and raises a sharper
question; someone reacts to the player's digging. Ends on a reveal, a threat, or a door that shouldn't
be unlocked.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the three acts above, each with its act-ending reveal.
- `## Pressure` — the dated deadline(s) and the pursuit escalation.
- `## Opposition` — the network, its off-screen mover, and what it does each act.
- Add `## Clue Chain` — reveal → where it is planted → what it reframes (keeps foreshadowing honest).
- `## Presence Matrix` — rotating, full cast only at the climax beat.

## Golden rules it keeps / swaps
Keeps the classic set: *thriller, not hangout* — every turn turns, a clock ticks, the mystery drips.

## Pitfalls
- Dumping the truth in a document instead of letting the player assemble it.
- A villain who only exists in the finale — show the network acting from act 1.
- Too many red herrings: every false lead must still teach the player something true.
