# Structure · Siege & Survival — hold the line while everything runs out
cast_pattern: revolving

A place must be held — a walled town, a stranded station, a shelter in a long winter — against
something that will not leave. The story is a sequence of hard allocations: who eats, who stands
watch, what gets sacrificed. Hope is a resource like any other.

## Phase shape (4 stages)
1. **The walls close** — the threat arrives; the player takes stock of people, supplies and weak points.
2. **Holding** — rounds of attrition: a raid, a shortage, a quarrel, a stranger at the gate. Each round
   spends something and forces a choice.
3. **The breach** — something fails (a wall, a well, a leader, trust). The plan for holding is gone.
4. **The last stand or the break-out** — defend what remains, or lead people out through the danger.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**Dwindling resources** instead of a calendar: 3–4 counters (food, defenders, morale, fuel or medicine)
that each round spends. The question is never "when" but "what do we give up next."

## Opposition
An overwhelming outside force — an army, a horde, a storm, a sickness, the cold itself — with internal
strife as the second front: a faction that wants to surrender, hoard, or flee.

## Endings it supports
Relief arrives, at a cost · break-out and exodus · surrender on negotiated terms · the place falls and
survivors scatter · the besieger collapses from its own pressures.

## Cast pattern — revolving door
A few constants (a co-leader, a medic, a stubborn elder) and a stream of others who arrive, fall,
leave, or turn. The depth check is **turnover with weight**: newcomers keep arriving through the
middle stages, some departures are losses the player feels, and at least one constant changes sides
or breaks.

## What a good beat looks like
A choice with a face on each side — two people who need the same last thing. Spend a resource, show
who it cost, end on the next sound from beyond the wall.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the four stages; list 4–6 holding rounds with their dilemma.
- `## Pressure` — the resource counters, starting values, and what happens at zero (mirror them into
  `state.seed.json` flags so the GM tracks them).
- `## Opposition` — the outside force's escalation, round by round, and the inside faction.
- `## Presence Matrix` — constants recur; mark arrivals and departures per beat.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *A clock ticks* → **The larder empties.** Show the counters through fiction, never as a HUD.
- *Failure is a story* stays, but **losses are permanent** — fail forward means the story continues,
  not that the dead come back.

## Pitfalls
- Repetitive raids — vary each round's dilemma (moral, social, physical, strange).
- Despair without agency: every round must offer a real choice, including a hopeful one.
- Bookkeeping crowding the prose — mention counters only when they change.
