# Structure · Expedition into the Unknown — the map fills in, and so do we
cast_pattern: fixed-party

A team goes where no map is reliable — a sunken continent, an uncharted ice field, a derelict ship,
the deep levels of a ruin, an unexplored planet. Each step forward is a discovery and a cost. What
they find at the heart is never what anyone hoped, and the way back is its own story.

## Phase shape (5 stages)
1. **Outfitting** — why go, who comes, what to carry. Every choice here matters later.
2. **The threshold** — leaving the known: the last outpost, the last radio contact.
3. **Deep in** — discoveries, wonders, losses. Supplies and nerves fray.
4. **The heart** — what was sought, and what it actually is.
5. **The return** — getting back, carrying something (or someone, or a truth) that changes things.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**Supplies and distance from safety** — a point of no return the player can see coming — plus team
condition and a **weather or season window** that will close.

## Opposition
Nature and the environment, the unknown itself (strange rules, a presence), a rival expedition, or the
team's own fracture as fear and ambition pull members apart.

## Endings it supports
Return with the prize, at a cost · return empty-handed but changed · stay (the unknown claims or
welcomes them) · the find is something no one wanted · the expedition becomes the first of many.

## Cast pattern — fixed party
The team is together nearly all the time. The depth check is **spotlight and fracture**: each member
has a beat where their expertise or flaw decides the outcome, and at least one disagreement splits the
team in a beat or two.

## What a good beat looks like
A discovery that answers one question and raises a stranger one; a choice about risk (the faster
dangerous route, the rest the team needs, the sample worth the weight). End on a sound, a sign, a gap
in the map.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the five stages; which discoveries happen where.
- Add `## The Map` — zones: known · rumored · unknown; what each holds.
- Add `## The Team` — member · expertise · reason for coming · breaking point.
- `## Pressure` — supply counters, the point of no return, the window.
- `## Opposition` — the environment's escalation and any rival or presence.
- `## Presence Matrix` — full team by default; mark split-off and solo beats.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *The mystery drips* → **The map fills in.** Each discovery is a reveal; keep one blank always ahead.
- *Every character is a gap* → **curiosity, duty and ambition** drive the team.

## Pitfalls
- A travelogue without decisions — every zone needs a choice, not only a view.
- Monsters of the week; give the environment consistent rules the team can learn.
- Faceless nature: let the unknown have patterns, moods and a sense of attention.
