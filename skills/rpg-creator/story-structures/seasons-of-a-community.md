# Structure · Seasons of a Community — slice of life, with purpose
cast_pattern: rotating

The player takes on a place and its people — an inn, a farm, a clinic, a ferry, a workshop, a small
town — and lives through its seasons. Nothing explodes. Stakes are small and personal: a project to
finish, a resident to help, a festival to make happen. The pleasure is watching a place come alive.

## Phase shape (seasons; one year or more)
Each **season** is a phase with three parts: a community **need** (a leaking roof, a lonely neighbor, a
failed harvest), a **project** milestone the player is building toward, and a **festival or ritual**
that closes it. 4 seasons for a year; repeat with changes for a second year.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**The calendar, gently:** the festival date, the harvest, the first snow. Plus a **project meter** (the
inn's repairs, the garden's yield, the clinic's supplies) and relationships that drift if neglected.
No doom clock. Missing a deadline changes the next season; it does not end anything.

## Opposition
None, or mild and human: weather, bureaucracy, an outside buyer, a rival business, a resident's own
stubbornness. Never a villain to defeat.

## Endings it supports
Quiet continuity (life goes on, richer) · a festival culmination · the place saved or transformed ·
the protagonist chooses to stay (or to leave with blessings).

## Cast pattern — rotating ensemble
Residents have routines (where they are by season and time of day), so who the player meets rotates
naturally. The ensemble-depth gate applies, but the "full cast" moment is each **festival**, not a
single climax — allow one full-cast gathering per season.

## What a good beat looks like
A small change of state: a resident shares one true thing, a project advances, a habit shifts. Ends on
a warm pull — an invitation, a question about tomorrow, a hint of next season's need.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — one line per season: need · project milestone · festival.
- Add `## Residents` — routine and want per resident (the GM uses it to place them).
- Add `## Projects` — what the player can build or restore, and what each milestone unlocks.
- `## Pressure` — the calendar dates and the project meter (mirror into `clock` and `flags`).
- `## Opposition` — the mild frictions, or "none".
- `## Presence Matrix` — rotating by routine; festival beats are the full-cast beats.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *Thriller, not hangout* → **Hangout with purpose.** Every scene changes something small; tension is
  low and that is deliberate.
- *End on a hook* → **End on a warm pull**, not a threat.
- *A clock ticks* → **The seasons turn.**

## Pitfalls
- Nothing happens: give every resident a want and every season a need.
- A management spreadsheet: resources are background; people are foreground.
- Conflict-free blandness: small disagreements and changing relationships are the drama.
