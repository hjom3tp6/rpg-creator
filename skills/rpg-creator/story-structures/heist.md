# Structure · Heist — the job, the crew, the plan, and how it breaks
cast_pattern: fixed-party

A crew of specialists sets out to take something guarded. The fun is competence under pressure: a
clever plan, then the moment reality departs from it and the crew improvises. The prize is rarely the
whole point — each member wants something the job can give or take away.

## Phase shape (5 movements)
1. **The job** — the target, the prize, the reason it must be now. The player decides to take it.
2. **The crew** — recruit or reunite specialists; each comes with a skill, a price, and a secret.
3. **Legwork** — scouting, forging, cultivating an inside contact. Every prep beat yields an asset or
   a liability (a debt, a witness, a suspicious guard).
4. **The execution** — the plan meets reality. Complications arrive in order; the crew splits up.
5. **The fallout** — the real plan or the double-cross is revealed; who walks away with what.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**The window** — a single night, event or transfer when the job is possible — plus **heat**: every
visible prep action raises suspicion (track it as a flag or counter). High heat closes doors and moves
the window.

## Opposition
An institution's security (locks, guards, procedure) given a face by one sharp individual — the owner,
the investigator, the rival crew. Secondary: the crew's own trust.

## Endings it supports
Clean getaway with a hidden cost · the real score was something else · caught, but the crew chose each
other · the crew fractures and the player chooses a side · walk away from the prize.

## Cast pattern — fixed party
The crew is present together for planning and fallout. The depth check is **spotlight**, not rotation:
each member owns at least one execution beat where only their skill and stake decide it, and each
secret pays off once.

## What a good beat looks like
A plan step, a complication, a crew member's skill or stake that resolves it — at a cost the player
chooses. Planning beats end on a doubt; execution beats end mid-motion.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the five movements; mark which prep beats feed which execution step.
- `## Pressure` — the window and the heat track (what raises it; what each level closes).
- `## Opposition` — the security layers and the face behind them.
- Add `## The Crew` (member · skill · stake · secret) and `## The Plan` (step · what will go wrong).
- `## Presence Matrix` — full crew in planning; pairs and solos during execution.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *Thriller, not hangout* → **Competence is the fantasy.** Let the crew be brilliant, then make it go
  sideways.
- *The mystery drips* → **The player knows the plan; tension lives in the deviation.**

## Pitfalls
- Endless planning — cap legwork at 3–5 beats, then go.
- A plan that simply works. Something must break, and the fix must cost.
- A twist that retroactively voids the player's choices — reveal hidden layers, never erase decisions.
