# Structure · Revenge — the list, and what each name costs
cast_pattern: solo

Something was taken from the protagonist — a person, a home, a name. They know (or learn) who did it,
and work through a list of those responsible. Each step toward the goal costs a piece of who they
were. The last name on the list is not who they expected, and the final choice is theirs.

## Phase shape (5 stages)
1. **The wound** — what was taken; can be a playable prologue or a past the player uncovers.
2. **The list** — targets in order, each guarded differently, each teaching something.
3. **The cost** — each act of revenge damages something human: a friendship, a reputation, a vow.
4. **The source** — the final target reframes the wound (a bigger system, a loved one, oneself).
5. **The choice** — complete it, spare them, or turn the drive toward something else.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**Targets learn and move** after each strike (the trail cools, defenses rise), and a ledger of the
protagonist's **humanity spent** — the people and principles given up — that the GM keeps visible
through fiction.

## Opposition
The targets and their network; the protagonist's own conscience; and someone who loves them and tries
to stop them (a pursuer, an old friend, an investigator).

## Endings it supports
Completed at total cost · mercy at the last moment · justice through others instead · the cycle passes
to someone new · transformation (the drive becomes protection or rebuilding).

## Cast pattern — solo with visitors
The protagonist moves alone; each target and their circle are visitors for a stretch. The depth check
is **humanized visitors and a constant shadow**: each target has someone who loves them on stage, and
one recurring confidant or pursuer appears across at least three stages.

## What a good beat looks like
The target is humanized at the worst moment — a child's drawing on the desk, a kindness to a stranger.
The player chooses. Ends on the cost landing, not on triumph.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the five stages; the reframe placed in stage 4.
- Add `## The Wound` — what happened, who knows the full truth.
- Add `## The List` — target · role in the wound · what they have now · who they love.
- `## Pressure` — how each strike changes the remaining targets; the humanity ledger in `flags`.
- `## Opposition` — the network, the conscience, the one who tries to stop them.
- `## Presence Matrix` — mostly the protagonist plus one visitor group per stage; mark the shadow.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *Thriller, not hangout* stays, but **the cost lands before the triumph.**
- *Every character is a gap* → **guilt, vow and debt** drive the cast; targets are people, not loot.

## Pitfalls
- Glorified cruelty — the story is about the cost, not the gore.
- Monotone grimness — leave room for a friendship or a joke the protagonist almost allows.
- The list as a checklist: each target must change the plan, not just shorten the list.
