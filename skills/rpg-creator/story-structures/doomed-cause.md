# Structure · Doomed Cause — a tragedy chosen with open eyes
cast_pattern: fixed-party

A band commits to a cause the player knows (or soon learns) cannot fully win — a rebellion against an
empire, a last defense, a dying tradition. The tension is not whether it ends, but how, and what
survives it. Agency lives in meaning: what is saved, who is remembered, what is passed on.

## Phase shape (4 stages)
1. **The oath** — why the cause is worth it. Show what is beautiful about it and about the band.
2. **High water** — victories; for a while it seems possible. The band is at its best.
3. **The turn** — a betrayal, a defeat, or the true price comes due. Hope narrows.
4. **The last stand** — the choice of what to save, and the ending the band chooses to meet.

## Pressure (replaces the ticking clock)
**Inevitability:** a known end (overwhelming odds, a prophecy, a history the player has glimpsed) that
draws closer. Track a ledger of **what will be lost** and **what can still be saved**; player choices
move items between them.

## Opposition
An overwhelming force — an empire, a tide of history, a plague of change — with a human face who is
not purely evil. The cause's own flaws are a second opponent.

## Endings it supports
Tragic with meaning · legacy (something carried out by a survivor) · pyrrhic (the cause wins and
destroys itself) · the survivor's testimony · a sliver of hope that the next generation inherits.

## Cast pattern — fixed party
The band is together through most of the story. The depth check is **spotlight and loss**: each member
has a beat that is theirs alone, and each loss is set up by an earlier scene that made it matter.

## What a good beat looks like
A quiet moment weighed by what is coming — a meal, a promise, a song. Or a choice about what to save
with the time that remains. End on dread lightly held, or on warmth that will hurt later.

## Laying out main-arc.md
- `## Phases` — the four stages; mark where each member's fate is decided (by choices, not script).
- Add `## What Will Be Lost` and `## What Can Be Saved` — the two ledgers, mirrored into `flags`.
- `## Pressure` — the inevitability and its milestones.
- `## Opposition` — the force, its human face, and the cause's internal flaw.
- `## Presence Matrix` — mostly the full band; mark each member's solo spotlight beat.

## Golden rules it swaps
- *Failure is a story* → **Loss is the story; meaning is the player's.** Choices decide how and what,
  not whether. The player's own character still fails forward unless they ask for hardcore stakes.
- *Thriller, not hangout* → **The hangout is the point** — warmth is what makes the fall matter.

## Pitfalls
- Nihilism: misery without beauty is not tragedy. Keep the warmth.
- Railroaded doom: if no choice changes anything, players disengage — let them change the shape.
- Telegraphing every death the same way; vary how and when losses land.
